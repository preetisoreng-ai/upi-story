Method & source

A transparent monthly record.

Seven official NPCI financial-year workbooks were combined into one chronological series. Indian comma separators and stray whitespace were normalized. Exact duplicates alone were eligible for removal; none were found.

The audit found no missing months, conflicting records, nonnumeric totals, or nonpositive totals. One malformed comma grouping in April 2026 was flagged and retained after separator removal.
