NPCI UPI monthly data audit
===========================
Raw workbooks: 7 XLSX files plus source_notes.txt
Sheets: one sheet named Product Statistics in every workbook
Headers: Month; No. of Banks live on UPI; Volume (In Mn.); Value (In Cr.)
Units: banks live (count), transaction volume (millions), transaction value (INR crore)
Raw date format: full English month name and four-digit year (for example, March-2021)
Raw order: reverse chronological within each financial-year workbook
Covered period: 2020-04 to 2026-10
Monthly observations written: 79
Exact duplicate records removed: 0
Conflicting months: 0
Missing months: none
Nonnumeric values: 0
Nonpositive totals: 0

Issues and inconsistencies
--------------------------
- All workbook cells are stored as text rather than typed dates/numbers.
- Numeric comma formatting is inconsistent across files. Values generally use Indian grouping; volumes switch between ungrouped and grouped forms.
- March 2025 and April-June 2025 include trailing tab/whitespace characters in banks and volume cells; these are trimmed.
- April 2026 value is written as "29,029,88.05", which is malformed Indian grouping. Removing separators yields 2902988.05; the observation is retained and explicitly flagged rather than silently dropped.
- The source-notes download date and period remain placeholders and should be completed from the actual download record.
- The workbooks contain no embedded source footnotes. The accompanying source_notes.txt records the exclusion of transactions with debit and credit to the same account from August 2018 onward.
