# UPI story revision summary

## What changed

- The lead changed from transaction-count growth to the question the story now answers: whether average payment value increased or decreased.
- The revised headline states the verified endpoint result: the nominal monthly average fell from INR 1,512.06 in April 2020 to INR 1,238.06 in October 2026, a decrease of INR 274.00 or 18.1%.
- The main interactive chart now defaults to average payment value. Its y-axis and hover values both use INR.
- The supporting charts now show the full average-value series, count versus value growth on one indexed axis, and October-to-October comparisons.
- A twelve-check table was added to the page. It reports calculation, data-quality, date-choice, consistency, seasonality, unusual-month, growth-driver, month-length, payment-mix, inflation, and conclusion-strength checks.
- Findings, possible explanations, and things the dataset cannot establish are separated.
- The methods note now states the source, covered period, units, formula, nominal-value limitation, and unknown download date.

## Why it changed

The original claim that transaction count grew about 25 times is correct, but it does not answer the main question. The revised story leads with the average payment calculation and uses transaction-count growth only to explain why the aggregate average declined.

## Unresolved issues

- The local source note does not record the actual download date.
- The files do not independently confirm whether October 2026 was a complete reporting month.
- The national totals contain no payment-category, person-to-person/person-to-merchant, merchant-category, transaction-size, user-level, or inflation data.
- Because only totals are available, the analysis cannot estimate a median payment or describe individual behaviour.

The pre-edit story and charts are preserved in `archive/pre-review-2026-11-03/`.
