02 · What drives the average

Transaction count grew faster than value.

When count rises faster than total value, the average value per transaction falls. These are aggregate national totals, not evidence about individual behaviour.

05 · What we can conclude

The finding is clear. Its cause is not.

What the data establishes

The nominal monthly average was ₹274 lower in October 2026 than in April 2020, an 18.1% decrease. Count grew faster than total value, and the direction holds across alternative dates.

Possible explanations

A changing mix of payment categories, wider merchant acceptance, adoption patterns, or pandemic recovery could affect the series. This dataset cannot test those explanations.

What it cannot tell us

It does not show a median payment, individual behaviour, what people bought, person-to-person versus merchant payments, or an inflation-adjusted result.

Method & source

A transparent monthly record.

Seven official NPCI financial-year workbooks were combined into 79 monthly observations from April 2020 through October 2026. Volume is in millions of transactions; value is in INR crore. Average payment value is value_crore × 10 ÷ volume_million.

Indian comma separators and stray whitespace were normalized. No records were silently discarded. One malformed comma grouping in April 2026 was flagged and retained after separator removal.
