India's UPI went from 1 billion payments a month to 822 million a day.

79 months of official NPCI data in 40 seconds:
• 25.5× more monthly transactions, Apr 2020 → Oct 2026
• 80% of the 758 banks on UPI today joined after April 2020
• 31% of all UPI payments since April 2020 happened in the last 12 months
• The average October payment fell 34%, from ₹1,864 to ₹1,238

About this project
The UPI story is an independent data-visualisation project built entirely from public data: the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) monthly UPI product statistics, April 2020 to October 2026. Every number can be reproduced from the published source.

How it was made
I built it with my own approval-gated AI framework. A public dataset goes in; four reviewed artifacts come out, and a person approves each stage before the next one starts. The intro video and these posts come out of the same workflow.

① Public dataset in: NPCI monthly UPI statistics
   ↓ profile + context
② Original analysis: strongest defensible story
   ↓ human review + approval
③ Pressure-tested deep-dive: checks claims, limits
   ↓ human review + approval
④ Dashboard: validated measures only
   ↓ human review + approval
⑤ Data story web app: shared theme, live on GitHub
   ↓ final approval
⑥ Intro video + /post-social: right format per platform
